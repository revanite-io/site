24 days of Gemara, December 1 to 24. Each day brings a short workshop and a puzzle.

We start with a finished audit log and work backwards: each day you build one of the documents that feed it, from guidance and control catalogs to evaluation logs, until you write, validate and publish your own audit on [grc.store](https://grc.store).

Day 1 unlocks on December 1.
